# Common questions

**Why can't I post a listing, request or offer?**
Your account must be Active (not Suspended) and all your documents must be current. A Suspended account cannot post, offer or accept. A document that expired suspends the account automatically; upload a renewal on Profile and, once the admin verifies it, the suspension is lifted. Also check the form: kg above 0, a future use-by or needed-by time, a pickup window that ends before the use-by, 1 to 5 photos, and the attestation ticked.

**Why can't I choose "cooked" or "baked" food?**
Casual donors can post only raw and sealed-packaged food, because they have no Sanitary Permit. Business donors with both permits can also post packaged, baked and cooked food. A Casual donor with both permits can upgrade to Business from Profile.

**Can a food group post a listing?**
No. Food groups post requests and send offers on listings. Donors post listings and send offers on requests.

**How many photos do I need?**
1 to 5 for every listing, request, offer and report.

**What does the use-by time mean, and what is the pickup window?**
Use-by is the time the food must be picked up before. The pickup window is when pickup can happen, and it must end before the use-by. A request has a needed-by time instead of a use-by.

**What are the storage conditions?**
Ambient Dry (room temperature, 22 to 28 °C, sealed containers), Chilled Cold-Chain (4 °C or below, the group brings a cooler) and Hot Hold (above 60 °C, pick up within 90 minutes).

**Do I have to list allergens?**
Allergen labels are optional and informational: Gluten / Wheat, Dairy / Butter, Nuts / Peanuts, Eggs, Halal Ingredients.

**What can't be donated?**
Milk products, tomato- or milk-based cooked food, and food already served to diners. The donor attestation confirms the food is apparently wholesome, properly labeled and donated in good faith.

**Who can see my offer?**
Only the poster and the offerer. Competing offers are hidden from other donors.

**Can I offer for only part of a listing?**
No. A food group's offer on a listing is for the whole quantity. On a request, a donor may offer a smaller amount than requested, but not more.

**Can I send more than one offer on the same post?**
No. It is one offer per group per listing, and one per donor per request. After a decline you cannot offer again on that post.

**Can I take back my offer?**
Yes, withdraw it any time before the poster decides. Once the poster has accepted it, you cannot withdraw; only the poster can cancel.

**What happens when an offer is accepted?**
That offer becomes Accepted, all other Offered offers on the post are declined, and the post becomes Accepted. Both sides see each other's contact number, and the food group sees the donor's handover instructions.

**How do the handoff and receipt work?**
At pickup, the donor confirms release first (Confirm Release). Then the food group confirms receipt (Confirm Receipt). Receipt cannot be confirmed before release. Confirming receipt makes the offer Completed, the post Rescued, and creates the handoff record.

**What is the report and when is it due?**
After receipt, the food group submits one report per handoff within 7 days: the event or program, the people actually served, a short description and 1 to 5 photos. The donor can read it on the handoff record. If it is late, the admin is flagged, and the report can still be submitted ("submitted late"). An overdue report does not block posting and does not suspend the account automatically.

**What does Lapsed mean?**
An offer was accepted but no handoff happened within 2 hours after the pickup time (or the end of the pickup window for a request). The offer becomes Lapsed and the post becomes Expired. To try again, the poster makes a new post.

**What does Expired mean?**
For a post: its use-by or needed-by time passed, or the accepted pickup was missed. For an offer: the pickup time passed with no answer, or the request expired.

**Can I cancel a post?**
The poster can cancel an Open or Accepted post, with a reason, but not after the handoff is complete. The offerers see the reason.

**Why can't I see a listing or request?**
Items from a Suspended account are hidden until it is reinstated. Casual donors see only requests that accept raw or sealed-packaged food. Items in your own city are listed first, with other cities below.

**Who sees my phone number?**
Contact numbers are shown only to the two sides of an accepted offer, until the handoff closes.

**How do I register, and how long does approval take?**
Use Register on the sign-in screen, fill in the form and upload the documents for your role. Your registration is Pending until the admin checks it; you get an email either way. I cannot tell how long it will take. Approval emails a temporary password.

**I forgot my password. What do I do?**
Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in screen. A new temporary password is emailed if the email has an account (the screen answers the same either way). A temporary password only lets you reach Change Password until you set a new one.

**My registration was rejected.**
The admin must give a reason, which is emailed to you. You may register again.

**A document is about to expire.**
A warning shows 30 days or less before the expiry date. Upload the renewal on Profile before it expires. If it expires, the account is Suspended until a renewal is verified.

**How do I report a safety problem?**
Either side of an Accepted or Completed offer can flag a safety concern about the other. The other party and the admin are notified. The admin dismisses the notice or suspends the account responsible.

**Is the handoff record an official certificate?**
No. It is an ANIPLATO platform record, not a certificate issued by DSWD, the Philippine Red Cross or any government agency. ANIPLATO grants no legal protection.

**Can you check my account, approve me, or post for me?**
No. I only explain how ANIPLATO works. I cannot see accounts or change anything. To check your own status, look at your Profile and your dashboard, or ask an admin.

**(Admin) What should I check before approving a registration?**
Every document must be unexpired and match the organization's name and city. A Casual donor's Barangay Certificate is checked against name and city. The phone number is entered, not verified. To reject, a reason is required and is emailed to the applicant.

**(Admin) What can I do about a safety notice?**
Dismiss it, or suspend the account responsible, with a note. Suspending needs a written reason. You can reinstate an account later.

**(Admin) How are the rates calculated?**
Rescue rate = kg rescued divided by kg posted over closed posts, leaving out cancelled ones (a request filled for less than asked counts only the accepted offer's kg). Offer acceptance rate = posts with an accepted offer divided by posts with at least one offer. Lapse rate = lapsed offers divided by accepted offers.
