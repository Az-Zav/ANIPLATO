# Rules and statuses

## Food rules

- Business donors may post raw, packaged, baked and cooked food. Casual donors may post raw and sealed-packaged food only (they have no Sanitary Permit). There is no kg cap for Casual donors.
- The donor attestation says the food is apparently wholesome, properly labeled and donated in good faith, and contains no milk products, no tomato- or milk-based cooked food, and no food already served to diners.
- The group attestation says the food will be given away free, will not be sold, and will be handled safely after pickup. Photos show no identifiable children, and identifiable adults have consented.
- Storage condition is required: **Ambient Dry** (room temperature, 22 to 28 °C, sealed containers), **Chilled Cold-Chain** (4 °C or below; the group brings a cooler) or **Hot Hold** (above 60 °C; pick up within 90 minutes).
- Allergen labels are optional and informational: Gluten / Wheat, Dairy / Butter, Nuts / Peanuts, Eggs, Halal Ingredients.
- Handover instructions stay hidden until the offer is accepted.
- Every post, offer and report needs 1 to 5 photos.
- A Casual donor sees, and can offer on, only requests that accept raw or sealed-packaged food, and only in those categories.

## Number and time rules

- Listing: kg above 0, use-by in the future, pickup window ends before the use-by.
- Request: kg above 0, needed-by in the future, event date on or after the needed-by.
- Food groups cannot post listings or re-post.

## Documents

- Every document needs an expiry date ("No expiry" only where the document truly has none; permits and the Barangay Certificate always need a date).
- 30 days or less before a document expires, a warning is shown. When a document expires, the account becomes Suspended automatically (reason "Document expired"). Uploading a renewal that the admin verifies lifts that suspension.

## Account status

An account is either **Active** or **Suspended**.
- A Suspended account cannot post, send an offer or accept an offer. Its Open posts are hidden from the other side and its Offered offers are hidden from posters until it is reinstated.
- Everything else still works for a Suspended account: finishing handoffs already accepted, submitting reports, flagging concerns, uploading renewals and updating the profile.
- Active to Suspended: the admin suspends with a written reason, or a document expires. Suspended to Active: the admin reinstates, or a verified renewal is uploaded for a document-expiry suspension.
- An overdue report does not block posting or offering.

## Safety notices

Either side of an Accepted or Completed offer can flag a safety concern about the other. The other party and the admin are notified. The admin dismisses the notice or suspends the account responsible, with a note. An overdue report also raises a notice.

## Statuses

**Listing or request:**
- **Open**: waiting for offers.
- **Accepted**: the poster accepted an offer. It takes no new offers.
- **Rescued**: the handoff is complete.
- **Expired**: the use-by or needed-by passed, or the pickup was missed (pickup time + 2 hours with no handoff).
- **Cancelled**: the poster cancelled with a reason.

**Offer:**
- **Offered**: sent, waiting for the poster.
- **Withdrawn**: the offerer took it back before the poster decided.
- **Declined**: the poster declined it, accepted another, or cancelled the post.
- **Accepted**: the poster chose it; contact numbers are shared.
- **Completed**: both sides confirmed the handoff.
- **Lapsed**: accepted, but no handoff within 2 hours after the pickup time (or pickup window end).
- **Expired**: the pickup time passed with no answer, or the request expired.
- **Cancelled**: the poster cancelled the post after accepting.

**Registration:** Pending (waiting for the admin), Approved, Rejected (with a reason; may register again).

## Who can do what

- Post a listing: Business donor, Casual donor (raw and sealed-packaged only). Post a request: food group.
- Offer on a request: Business and Casual donors. Offer on a listing: food group.
- Accept or decline offers on your own post, and cancel your own post: whoever posted it.
- Withdraw your own offer: whoever sent it, until the poster decides.
- Confirm release: donors. Confirm receipt and submit the report: food group.
- Flag a safety concern, upload renewals, edit the profile: any donor or food group.
- Upgrade to Business: Casual donors.
- Approve or reject registrations, dismiss notices, suspend and reinstate accounts, and view the metrics: admin only.

## Automatic checks

When a screen loads (the first screen after signing in, then at most about once a minute), ANIPLATO lapses missed pickups, expires old listings, requests and offers, flags overdue reports to the admin, shows document-expiry warnings, and suspends accounts with expired documents.

## Admin reports

- **Rescue rate**: kg rescued divided by kg posted, over closed posts, leaving out cancelled ones. A request filled for less than it asked counts only the accepted offer's kg, so the shortfall lowers the rate.
- **Offer acceptance rate**: posts with an accepted offer divided by posts with at least one offer.
- **Lapse rate**: lapsed offers divided by accepted offers.
- Reports summarize listed, rescued, expired and cancelled food.
