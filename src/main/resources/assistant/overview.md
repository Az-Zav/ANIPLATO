# What ANIPLATO is

ANIPLATO is a surplus-food board for Metro Manila. It connects people with extra food to food groups that can use it. It does not deliver food, sell food, show maps or send in-app notifications. Emails are sent for registration results and temporary passwords.

## The roles

- **Business donor**: a business with a Mayor's Permit and a Sanitary Permit. Can post listings in these categories: raw, packaged, baked and cooked.
- **Casual donor**: a person or household without permits. Can post raw and sealed-packaged food only.
- **Food group**: an organization with a standing feeding activity (a feeding program, parish pantry, school feeding or volunteer group). One-off individual volunteers are not accepted. Posts requests and offers on listings.
- **Admin**: checks registrations, handles safety notices, suspends and reinstates accounts, and views the reports.

A role is fixed at registration. A Casual donor who later gets both permits can upgrade to Business by uploading the permits (the admin reviews it like a registration).

## The two routes

- **Route 1, listing**: a donor posts a listing. Food groups send offers on it. The donor accepts one.
- **Route 2, request**: a food group posts a request. Donors send offers on it. The food group accepts one.

Items in your own city appear first. Items from other cities are listed below them. Nothing is hidden, and offers can be sent across cities.

## The big picture

1. Register and get checked by the admin.
2. Post (a listing or a request).
3. Offer (the other side sends offers).
4. Accept (the poster accepts one offer).
5. Handoff (the donor confirms release, then the food group confirms receipt; the food group then submits a report).
6. Close (the post ends as Rescued, Expired or Cancelled).

Safety notices and suspensions can happen at any time after an offer is accepted.

## What ANIPLATO is not

ANIPLATO grants no legal protection. The handoff record is an ANIPLATO platform record, not a certificate issued by DSWD, the Philippine Red Cross or any government agency.
