# Step by step

## 1. Register and get verified

- Everyone fills one form: name, city, contact person, contact number and email. A Casual donor enters their own or household name, and their own name as contact person.
- Pick Donor or Food group. A donor then picks "I have a Mayor's Permit and Sanitary Permit" (Business) or "I don't have permits" (Casual).
- Upload the documents for the role, each with its expiry date, and agree to the consent text.
- Required documents: Business donor = Mayor's Permit and Sanitary Permit. Casual donor = one Barangay Certificate with an expiry date. Food group = one credential (DSWD, PCNC, SEC non-stock, DepEd/CHED/TESDA, or a diocese endorsement for parishes) plus an authorization letter naming the contact person.
- No government IDs are collected. Files are visible only to the applicant and the admin.
- The registration is Pending and a "Received" email is sent. The admin approves only if every document is unexpired and matches the name and city. Approval creates the account and emails a temporary password. Rejection needs a reason, which is emailed, and the applicant may register again.
- The temporary password never expires, but signing in with it reaches only Change Password until a new password is set. "Forgot password" emails a new temporary password, and the screen answers the same whether or not the email has an account.
- A business without both permits is not rejected. It registers as Casual.

## 2. Post

**A donor posts a listing (Route 1).** Needs an Active account with current documents. Enter the description, category, kg, use-by time and pickup window, and pick a storage condition. Upload 1 to 5 photos. Allergen labels and handover instructions are optional. Tick the donor attestation. The listing is then Open.

**A food group posts a request (Route 2).** Needs an Active account with current documents. Describe the food needed (required, up to 200 characters; it is the request's title everywhere), pick the categories you will accept, enter kg needed and a needed-by date and time, name the event or program (name, date, who it feeds and how many), and give the place where the food should go. Upload 1 to 5 photos of past events or the venue (or of the recipients if it is a new event). A message is optional. Tick the group attestation. The request is then Open. A group can have several Open requests at once.

## 3. Send offers

**A food group offers on a listing.** One offer per group per listing, for the whole quantity (no partial amounts). State how the food will be used, who benefits and how many, the place and a pickup time. The pickup time must be inside the listing's pickup window and before the use-by. Upload 1 to 5 photos and tick the group attestation. A group can withdraw until the donor decides.

**A donor offers on a request.** One offer per donor per request. Describe the food, pick its category and storage condition, add optional allergen labels, enter kg, the use-by time and a pickup window. The kg must be above 0 and not above the requested kg (a smaller amount is allowed). The pickup window must end before both the use-by and the request's needed-by. The category must be one the request accepts. Upload 1 to 5 photos and tick the donor attestation. A donor can withdraw until the food group decides.

Only the poster and the offerer can see an offer. An unanswered offer expires.

## 4. Accept an offer

- The poster (the donor on a listing, the food group on a request) looks at the offers and photos and accepts one. They can also decline any single offer.
- Accepting one offer declines all the other Offered offers on that post. The post becomes Accepted. This all happens together, or not at all.
- Both sides then see each other's contact number, and the food group sees the donor's handover instructions. Contact numbers are shown only to the two sides of the accepted offer.
- A declined offerer cannot offer again on that post. Once accepted, the offerer cannot withdraw; only the poster can cancel.

## 5. Handoff and report

- At pickup, the donor confirms release first. Then the food group confirms receipt. Neither step needs photos.
- Confirming receipt makes the offer Completed and the post Rescued, and creates the handoff record (names, food, agreed kg, times and stated use). The record shows in both histories.
- The food group then submits one report per handoff, due 7 days after receipt is confirmed. It holds the event or program name and date (prefilled), the people actually served, a short description, and 1 to 5 photos, with the photo consent ticked.
- The donor sees the report on the handoff record, read-only. The donor does not approve it. The report never holds up closing the post.
- If a report is overdue, the admin is flagged. The admin dismisses the flag or suspends the account with a note. There is no automatic suspension, and nothing is blocked. The group can still submit late; the flag then shows "submitted late".

## 6. Close

- The poster can cancel an Open or Accepted post with a reason, but not after the handoff is complete. Offerers affected see the reason.
- If an Accepted offer has no handoff 2 hours after its pickup time (listing) or pickup window end (request), the offer becomes Lapsed and the post becomes Expired. To try again, the poster makes a new post; nothing carries over.
- An Open post expires when its use-by (listing) or needed-by (request) passes; its Offered offers expire with it.
