# Where to find things

The sidebar shows only what the signed-in role can use. Profile is available to everyone. The user's current screen is given to you with each question.

## Donor sidebar
- **Dashboard**: an overview of the donor's activity.
- **New Listing**: post surplus food.
- **My Listings**: the donor's own listings. Each row has buttons such as Review offers (to accept or decline an offer), Confirm Release (once an offer is accepted, at pickup) and cancel. A badge shows offers waiting for a decision.
- **Browse Requests**: requests from food groups. Open one to send an offer.
- **My Offers**: offers the donor sent on requests, and their status. When a food group accepts one, the donor confirms release here at pickup (Confirm Release). A declined offer shows Offer again.
- **History**: closed listings and offers, and the handoff records.
- **Profile**: organization details, documents and renewals, and password.

## Food group sidebar
- **Dashboard**: an overview of the group's activity.
- **Browse Listings**: listings from donors. Open one to send an offer.
- **My Offers**: offers the group sent on listings, and their status. After the donor confirms release, the group confirms receipt here (Confirm Receipt). A badge shows handoffs waiting for pickup or receipt.
- **New Request**: post a request for food.
- **My Requests**: the group's own requests. Each row has buttons such as Review offers (to accept or decline an offer), Confirm Receipt (after the donor confirms release) and cancel.
- **History**: closed requests and offers, and the handoff records.
- **Profile**: organization details, documents and renewals, and password.

The food group's **Submit Report** form is not in the sidebar. A "Submit report →" banner appears when a report is due, and the Handoff Record (opened from History) has a Submit Report button.

## Admin sidebar
- **Dashboard**: summary counts.
- **Pending Accounts**: registrations and upgrades waiting for review. Open one to check the documents, then approve or reject (a reason is required to reject).
- **All Accounts**: every account and its status. The admin can suspend (with a reason) or reinstate.
- **Safety Notices**: concerns flagged by users, and overdue-report flags. The admin dismisses a notice or suspends the account responsible, with a note.
- **Reports**: the metrics (rescue rate, offer acceptance rate, lapse rate and totals).
- **Profile**.

## Other places
- **Log Out** is at the bottom of the sidebar and asks for confirmation first.
- The header shows the organization's name (or Admin).
- The floating button in the corner opens this assistant.
- On the sign-in screen: Log In, Register, and Forgot password.
